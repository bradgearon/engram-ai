# External Resource Notes

## Open Music Theory
https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/

Best chapters to pull for a first musician wind:
- Fundamentals (intervals, triads) — only if the wind lacks them
- Diatonic and chromatic harmony (for reharmonization transforms)
- Form (sentence, period, small forms)
- Popular music and jazz units if the target genres need them

Use for additional graded exercises and counter-examples. Do not let it override the Interval Emotion Field or AHD rules.

## Toby Rush — Music Theory for Musicians and Normal People
https://tobyrush.com/theorypages/

One-page posters. Excellent for quick, dense concept cards that can become retrieval snippets or heart constraints.

## Alan Belkin
https://alanbelkinmusic.com/

Free books on Harmony, Counterpoint, Orchestration, and Composition. Particularly useful:
- Musical ideas and how they are developed
- Form as something heard over time
- Orchestration as composing with timbre and supporting form (not just assigning colors)

Good source of “why” explanations that can become critique language for the heart.

## musictheory.net & Teoria
- https://www.musictheory.net/
- https://www.teoria.com/

Use for generating or validating basic interval/chord examples. Secondary to the local emotional and AHD materials.

## Signals Music Studio
https://signalsmusic.studio/lessons

Practical modern harmony, modes, voice leading, and “how this actually appears in songs.” Useful for pop/rock/metal-adjacent trajectories.

## Usage rule

External material is optional enrichment. The authoritative emotional, AHD, motif-development, and resonator rules remain the local files in this package and the original Resone attachments.
