# Music Theory & Composition Learning Package for Wind Training

## Purpose

This package collects high-signal sources and distilled learnings for training musician / artist / producer winds. It is built around the Resone materials you already have plus curated external references.

The goal is not a general music-theory dump. It is material that can be turned into:

- structured practice trajectories
- constraint checkers for a heart evaluator
- small wind policy targets (motif development, AHD, interval emotion, genre form, drum grammar)
- held-out transfer tasks

## How to use this package

1. Read `00_INDEX.md` (this file) and `01_TRAINING_INSTRUCTIONS.md`.
2. Use the distilled `.md` files in `extracted/` as the seed knowledge for a wind.
3. Feed the external URLs (listed below) to an LLM or scraper only after you have decided the held-out tasks.
4. Emit candidate `policy.wind` packages only when held-out music tasks improve.

## Core local materials (already in your attachments)

- `resone_song_design_genre_guide.md` — form, motif development, density, climax, AHD integration by genre
- `resone_drums_genre_guide.md` — drum grammar, anchors, fills, energy moves
- `interval_emotion_field_guide.md` — per-interval emotional field (core, ascending, continuation, register, etc.)
- `anchored_harmonic_divergence.md` — AHD: keep home while developing a dissonant space
- `composition-tips.md` — statements/answers/counters as positional anchors; handoff commitments
- `resonator_api_v0.1.md` + `music-composition.json` — output contract and lane rules

## High-value external URLs

### Open textbooks and systematic theory
- Open Music Theory (v2): https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/
- Toby Rush – Music Theory for Musicians and Normal People: https://tobyrush.com/theorypages/
- musictheory.net: https://www.musictheory.net/
- Teoria: https://www.teoria.com/

### Craft and form (especially useful for winds)
- Alan Belkin – free online books (Harmony, Counterpoint, Orchestration, Composition): https://alanbelkinmusic.com/
  - Direct PDF examples often linked from the site (Orchestration, etc.)

### Practical / modern
- Signals Music Studio (Jake Lizzio) – free lessons on progressions, modes, voice leading: https://signalsmusic.studio/lessons
- 12tone YouTube – clear explanations of harmony and form
- 8-bit Music Theory YouTube – game music orchestration and form

### Ear and practice
- musictheory.net exercises
- Teoria dictation and interval tools
- Fifths.io tools: https://fifths.io/tools/

## Recommended reading order for a first musician wind

1. Interval Emotion Field Guide (local) — gives the wind a concrete emotional vocabulary
2. Anchored Harmonic Divergence (local) — one high-leverage technique
3. Song Design Genre Guide excerpts (local) — form and development rules for 3–4 target genres
4. Composition tips (statements / answers / counters)
5. Open Music Theory chapters on form + motif
6. Alan Belkin on musical ideas and form
7. Drum grammar only if the wind will touch percussion lanes

## What a good wind package should be able to do after training

- Given a short motif and a target emotion/interval, produce a continuation that uses the Continuation property from the Interval Guide
- Apply AHD: establish home, open a dissonant space, make both desirable, return with payoff
- Transform rather than replace material (octave, fragmentation, reharmonization, density change)
- Respect genre-level form expectations without erasing user constraints
- Produce resonator-legal output when that is the target notation

See `01_TRAINING_INSTRUCTIONS.md` for a concrete training plan.
